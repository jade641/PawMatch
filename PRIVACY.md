Privacy Policy for PawMatch

Last updated: October 20, 2026

PawMatch ("the App", "we", "us") is a mobile application developed as a student capstone project at the University of Mindanao. It helps dog owners find suitable matches for their dogs. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google or email:

Your name
Your email address
Your public profile picture

When you sign in with Google, we only receive your basic profile (name, email address, and profile picture). We do not access any other data in your Google account.

Information you add in the App:

Your dog's profile (name, breed, age, sex, photos, and description)
Your matches and activity within the App
2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

Create and manage your account
Show your dog's profile to other users for matchmaking
Let you view and manage your matches
Improve and test the App as part of our academic project
3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your dog's profile and the name you use in the App may be visible to other App users for matchmaking purposes. We use third-party services (Supabase for authentication and database storage, and Google Sign-In) to operate the App. These services process data only as needed to provide their functions.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers. We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions: https://github.com/jade641/PawMatch/blob/main/DATA_DELETION.md

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
