# PawMatch: Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

If you want your PawMatch account and all data linked to it deleted, follow the steps below.

## What will be deleted

- Your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google or email login
- Your dog profiles and photos
- Your matches and other activity in the App

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **jcanlubopaye@gmail.com** with the subject **"PawMatch Data Deletion Request"**.
2. In the email, include the email address you used to log in to PawMatch, so we can find your account.
3. We will delete your data within **7 days** and send you a confirmation email once it is done.

## Questions

Contact us at **jcanlubopaye@gmail.com**.
